# 第一讲 slide plan：市场和经济学思维

Source: reverse engineered from `slides/第一讲-市场和经济学思维.html`.

Status: this is a planning document for future slide edits. It records the current deck's story chain, visible text, layout, visual role, and places where content can later be expanded. The HTML remains the presentation source for now.

## Deck Logic

The deck does not begin by defining "market". It first makes market exchange look strange, then asks what institutional conditions make it possible.

Story chain:

1. Market is familiar, but should be problematized.
2. Strange cases show that markets can turn non-obvious objects into tradable objects.
3. From those cases, define market as an institutionalized exchange order.
4. Ask where market boundaries come from.
5. Introduce spontaneous order / unintended social outcomes through Smith, Mandeville, and Hayek.
6. Translate that observation into the micro-macro problem.
7. Introduce the economic analysis template.
8. Apply the template to market mechanisms.
9. Discuss what it means to say markets are "effective".
10. Close by connecting market failure and course topics.

## Visual System

Canvas:
- 16:9 fixed deck, `1280 x 720`.
- Centered slide content with generous margins.
- Serif Chinese for main titles and body; sans/mono for labels.

Text hierarchy:
- H1: title-scale, used only for title slide.
- H2: large section / transition title.
- H3: main claim or question.
- H4: card-level claim.
- Body paragraph: lecture claim, not dense note prose.
- Small paragraph: explanation, caveat, source, or connective tissue.
- Eyebrow: mono label for slide role and section tracking.

Color roles:
- `dark`: opening, major turns, quotations, framing, final slides.
- `light`: concept explanation and tables.
- `cream`: classroom questions and softer explanatory pauses.
- `warm`: cases and examples.
- `terracotta`: strong transition / warning / conceptual pivot.
- `blue #527f99`: mechanisms, frameworks, market-level structure.

Blue slide convention:
- Background: `#527f99`.
- Card surface: `rgba(25,54,70,.24)`.
- Card label: `#F4B27B`.
- Card heading: white.
- Card body: warm white-gray.
- Keep blue slide surfaces in the same blue family; avoid white cards on blue.
